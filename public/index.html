Feedback-to-Roadmap Copilot

Messy feedback in, a prioritized, stakeholder-ready roadmap out.

1. Feedback Input

Paste raw feedback (one item per line works well - app reviews, survey responses, support tickets), or upload a .txt file.

2. Clustered Themes

Each theme is tagged with frequency (how often it comes up) and severity (how much it matters), scored independently. Every feedback item is classified into at most one theme's evidence - click "View supporting feedback" to see the full list, flag low-confidence classifications, and reassign an item if the AI got it wrong.

3. Prioritized Roadmap (RICE)

RICE score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort. Frequency is a fact about your feedback (how many items raised it) - Reach is the AI's judgment call about your wider user base, and they are deliberately not the same number. Every AI estimate is editable: edit any input below and the score recalculates instantly, and the cell is marked as a PM override once you do.
